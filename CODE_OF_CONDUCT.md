# Community expectations

Gradara welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and preferred tools. Ask clear questions, explain engineering disagreements with evidence, and critique the work rather than the person. Give others room to learn and correct mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, sharing private information without consent, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not spam issues with generated content or repeatedly disregard a maintainer's request to narrow a discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions when practical, apply expectations consistently, and keep private reports confidential to the people needed to address them. A person involved in a complaint should not decide it alone where another maintainer is available.

Report conduct concerns privately to Edgar Duarte at **[contact@virtu-services.us](mailto:contact@virtu-services.us?subject=Gradara%20conduct%20report)** with the subject **Gradara conduct report**, relevant links, and context. Do not post another person's private information in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security reporting procedure](SECURITY.md).

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained for Gradara. Contributors may ask for clarification or reconsideration of a moderation decision through the same private channel.
