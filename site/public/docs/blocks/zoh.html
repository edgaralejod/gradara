Zero-order hold

Sample and hold.

Description

Samples its input every Ts seconds and holds the value until the next sample. Use it to model an ADC or a sampled controller input.

Ports

Inputs

PortDomainDescription
usignalInput signal, read at each sample instant.

Outputs

PortDomainDescription
ysignalOutput signal, the last sample, constant between samples.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Sample period Ts0.001 s
≥ 0.0001		Sample period, in seconds. At least 0.0001 s (default 1 ms).

Equations

At t = k · Ts, k = 0, 1, 2, …: y = u(t)
Between samples, y holds its value.

Implementation

The block’s Modelica equations:

when sample(0, Ts) then
  y = u;
end when;

Assumptions and limitations

  • Samples are taken at fixed multiples of Ts from time 0. No quantization is modeled.
