Winding

Couples an electrical winding of N turns to a magnetic circuit.

Description

Couples an electrical winding of N turns to a magnetic circuit. The current drives a magnetomotive force N · i into the magnetic side, and the rate of change of flux induces the voltage on the electrical side. The coupling is lossless.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
+ pelectricalPositive electrical pin. Current into p is the winding current i.
− nelectricalNegative electrical pin.
m+ port_pmagneticPositive magnetic port. Positive i raises port_p above port_n, driving flux out of port_p through the external circuit.
m− port_nmagneticNegative magnetic port.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Turns N100
≥ 1		Number of turns, ≥ 1.

Equations

v = p.v − n.v, V_m = port_p.V_m − port_n.V_m
V_m = N · i
N · dΦ/dt = −v, with Φ = port_p.Φ (flux entering port_p)

Implementation

Instantiates Modelica.Magnetic.FluxTubes.Basic.ElectroMagneticConverter from the Modelica Standard Library 4.1.0. MSL documentation

Assumptions and limitations

  • No winding resistance or leakage inductance. Add a Resistor in series for copper loss.

Tips

  • With a reluctance R_m between port_p and port_n, the winding looks like an inductor of N² / R_m from the electrical side.
  • Every magnetic circuit needs a Magnetic ground.
