Block reference/Thermal

Fixed temperature

Holds its port at a constant temperature.

  • thermal
  • Modelica Standard Library
  • 1 port
  • 1 parameter

Description

Holds its port at a constant temperature, supplying or absorbing whatever heat flow that takes. Use it for ambient air, a coolant, or a heat sink held at a known temperature.

Example

Cooling a hot part A 10 W part cools through a thermal resistance into a heatsink that convects and radiates to 20 °C.
