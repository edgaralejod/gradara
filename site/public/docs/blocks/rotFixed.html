Fixed (rotational)

A flange fixed in the housing at a constant angle.

Description

Fixes a rotational flange to the housing at a constant angle. Use it as the reference for springs, dampers, and clutches that act against the housing.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
shaft flangerotationalFlange held at the fixed angle. It takes whatever reaction torque is needed.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription

Equations
