Battery

Battery stack: open-circuit voltage rising linearly with state of charge, internal resistance, starting full.

Description

A battery pack of Ns series by Np parallel identical cells. The open-circuit voltage rises linearly with state of charge (SOC) from OCVmin when empty to OCVmax when full, behind a fixed internal resistance.

The pack starts full (SOC = 1). SOC is the integral of the terminal current divided by the pack capacity.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
+ pelectricalPositive terminal. Current into p charges the pack; discharge current is negative.
− nelectricalNegative terminal.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Cells in series Ns96
≥ 1		Number of cells in series. Scales voltage and resistance.
Cells in parallel Np1
≥ 1		Number of cells in parallel. Scales capacity and divides resistance.
Cell capacity Q5 A·h
≥ 0		Capacity of one cell, in ampere-hours.
Cell voltage when full OCVmax4.2 V
≥ 0		Open-circuit voltage of one cell at SOC = 1, in volts.
Cell voltage when empty OCVmin2.5 V
≥ 0		Open-circuit voltage of one cell at SOC = 0, in volts. Must be below OCVmax.
Cell resistance Ri0.005 Ω
≥ 0		Internal resistance of one cell, in ohms.

Equations

i = p.i, p.i + n.i = 0
dSOC/dt = i / (Np · Q · 3600), SOC(0) = 1
OCV = Ns · (OCVmin + SOC · (OCVmax − OCVmin))
p.v − n.v = OCV + (Ns · Ri / Np) · i

Implementation

Instantiates Modelica.Electrical.Batteries.BatteryStacks.CellStack from the Modelica Standard Library 4.1.0. MSL documentation

Assumptions and limitations

  • SOC is held between 0 and 1 without stopping the run: an empty pack keeps supplying current at Ns · OCVmin, and charging a full pack has no effect on SOC.
  • No RC transient, temperature effects, self-discharge, or aging. The initial SOC cannot be changed.
