Magnetic ground

Zero magnetic potential reference.

Description

The zero of magnetic potential. Every magnetic circuit needs exactly one, as every electrical circuit needs a Ground.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
0 portmagneticMagnetic port, held at V_m = 0. Any flux can flow in or out.
