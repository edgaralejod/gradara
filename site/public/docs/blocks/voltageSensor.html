Voltage sensor

Measures voltage between two electrical nodes without loading the circuit.

Description

Measures the voltage between two nodes and outputs it as a signal. It draws no current, so it does not load the circuit.

Ports

Outputs

PortDomainDescription
V ysignal · VMeasured voltage p.v − n.v, in volts.

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
+ pelectricalPositive measuring pin.
− nelectricalNegative measuring pin.

Equations

y = p.v − n.v
p.i = 0
n.i = 0

Assumptions and limitations

  • Ideal: infinite input impedance, no bandwidth limit or noise.

Examples

Used in the shipped examples Buck converter · Ideal switches, EV drivetrain, 480 VAC → 24 VDC flyback. Open them from Examples in the app.
