Constant force

A constant force.

Description

Applies a constant force to the flange, independent of speed and direction. Use it for gravity on a vertical axis or a constant load.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
shaft flangetranslationalFlange the force acts on.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Force f_constant1 NThe force, in newtons. Positive accelerates the flange in the positive direction; negative acts as a load.

Equations

flange.f = −f_constant

Implementation

Instantiates Modelica.Mechanics.Translational.Sources.ConstantForce from the Modelica Standard Library 4.1.0. MSL documentation

Tips

  • For gravity on a mass m with positive direction upward, set f_constant = −9.81 · m.
