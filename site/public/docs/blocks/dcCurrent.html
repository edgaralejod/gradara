DC current

An ideal constant current source.

Description

An ideal constant current source.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
+ pelectricalPositive pin. The source current I flows into p, through the source, and out of n.
− nelectricalNegative pin. The current I leaves the source here into the external circuit.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Current I1 ACurrent, in amperes. Any real number.

Equations

p.i = I
p.i + n.i = 0

Implementation

Instantiates Modelica.Electrical.Analog.Sources.ConstantCurrent from the Modelica Standard Library 4.1.0. MSL documentation

Assumptions and limitations

  • No voltage limit: the source produces whatever voltage the load requires.

Tips

  • The direction follows the Modelica convention: a positive I pushes current out of n. Flip the pins, or the sign of I, if it runs the wrong way.
  • Never leave it open or in series with an ideal switch that can open; its voltage is then undefined.
