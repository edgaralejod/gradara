Block reference › Thermal

Heat capacitor

Stores heat: C·dT/dt = Q_flow.

Description

A lumped mass at one uniform temperature that stores heat. Its temperature rises with the net heat flowing into its port.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
T portthermalHeat port at the body temperature T, in kelvin. Heat flow into the port is positive and heats the body.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Heat capacity C1000 J/K
≥ 0		Heat capacity, in J/K: specific heat times mass (cp · m). Use a positive value.

Equations

C · dT/dt = port.Q_flow, with T = port.T

Implementation

Instantiates Modelica.Thermal.HeatTransfer.Components.HeatCapacitor from the Modelica Standard Library 4.1.0. MSL documentation

Assumptions and limitations

  • One lumped temperature: no gradients inside the body.
  • The initial temperature is the library default of 293.15 K (20 °C) and is not a parameter.

Tips

  • Connect it to Fixed temperature through a Thermal conductor for a first-order heat-up with time constant C / G.
