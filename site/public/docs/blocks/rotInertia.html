Inertia (ideal)

Rotating mass with two flanges and no losses.

Description

An ideal rotating mass with two flanges and no losses. Both flanges share the angle of the body. Use it for rotors, flywheels, and wheels, and add damping or friction with separate blocks.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
a flange_arotationalFlange a. Torque into the flange is positive.
b flange_brotationalFlange b, rigidly joined to flange_a.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Inertia J0.01 kg·m²
≥ 0		Moment of inertia, in kg·m². Zero or positive.

Equations

flange_a.phi = flange_b.phi = φ
ω = dφ/dt
J · dω/dt = flange_a.tau + flange_b.tau

Implementation

Instantiates Modelica.Mechanics.Rotational.Components.Inertia from the Modelica Standard Library 4.1.0. MSL documentation

Assumptions and limitations

  • No friction or damping to the housing. Add Torsion damper to Fixed (rotational) for viscous losses.
