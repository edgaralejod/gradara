Fixed (translational)

A translational flange fixed at a constant position.

Description

Fixes a translational flange at a constant position. Use it as the wall or frame that springs, dampers, and stops push against.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
shaft flangetranslationalFlange held at the fixed position. It takes whatever reaction force is needed.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Position s00 mThe fixed position, in meters.

Equations
