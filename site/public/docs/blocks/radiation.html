Radiation

Radiative exchange: Q = Gr·σ·(T_a⁴ − T_b⁴).

Description

Radiative heat exchange between two surfaces, following the Stefan–Boltzmann law. The heat flow grows with the difference of the fourth powers of the absolute temperatures.

Ports

Conserving terminals

PortDomainDescription
a port_athermalHeat port of surface a. Heat flow Q from a to b enters here.
b port_bthermalHeat port of surface b. Q leaves here.

Parameters

ParameterDefaultDescription
Net radiation conductance Gr0.01 m²
≥ 0		Net radiation conductance, in m²: an area weighted by emissivities and view factor. For a small body in large surroundings, Gr = ε · A.

Equations

Q = Gr · σ · (port_a.T⁴ − port_b.T⁴), σ = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴)
port_a.Q_flow = Q, port_b.Q_flow = −Q

Implementation

Instantiates Modelica.Thermal.HeatTransfer.Components.BodyRadiation from the Modelica Standard Library 4.1.0. MSL documentation

Assumptions and limitations

  • Gray, diffuse surfaces with a fixed Gr. No heat storage.

Tips

  • Temperatures are absolute; the ports must be in kelvin, as all thermal ports are.
