Privacy notice

Last updated 2026-09-28

This notice covers the Gradara desktop app, the Gradara AI service, and its sign-in page, operated by Virtu Services LLC, Wisconsin, USA ("we"). Questions or requests: support@virtu-services.us.

The short version

  • Your models, simulations, and results stay on your computer.
  • Apart from update checks, nothing is sent anywhere unless you use an AI feature. The simulation engine is built into the app and works offline.
  • The Gradara AI service does not store or log your prompts, models, equations, or AI responses. Our AI provider, Anthropic, may keep them briefly for abuse and safety monitoring under its terms.
  • We keep what accounts and billing need: your email, credit balance, purchases, and request counts.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking, in the app or on this website. We do not sell or share your personal information.

The desktop app

Models, simulation runs, results, exports, and your AI block library are saved in a data folder on your computer (Settings shows its location). Simulations run locally. Your own API keys and your Gradara AI sign-in are stored in the operating system keychain.

The app connects to the internet only to check GitHub for updates (at launch and every few hours; GitHub sees your IP address and the app version), to download an optional engine of your own if you choose one in Settings, to send AI requests when you ask for them, and to open links you click.

AI requests

An AI request contains your description and the model context the task needs, for example the component you are refining, the block catalog for a model build, or a controller's equations for C export. Editing or diagnosing the open model sends that model's blocks, parameters, equations, and connections without its layout, the names of selected blocks, and the block catalog; diagnosing a failed run also sends the problems you ask about, that run's generated Modelica source, and the solver's messages. It does not include unrelated models or files.

With your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, the request goes directly from your computer to that provider under your account and their terms. With Gradara AI, the request goes to our service, which forwards it to Anthropic and returns the result. Our service processes the content in memory only and does not write it to disk, logs, or its database. Anthropic processes it under its commercial API terms, which do not allow training on it, and may keep it for a limited period for abuse and safety monitoring.

Signing in

The sign-in page at api.gradara.app loads Google's Firebase Authentication scripts from Google servers, which handle sign-in with Google or an email link. Google receives your IP address, browser details, and email under its own policies. We never see passwords.

To stop abuse, the service limits how often one IP address can start a sign-in. It holds IP addresses for this only in memory, for about a minute, and never writes them to disk or logs.

Service providers

  • Google Cloud hosts the service and database in the United States (region us-central1).
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) handles sign-in with Google or an email link.

  • Firebase Hosting serves this website. We add no cookies or analytics to it.

Where data is processed

We are based in the United States, and Gradara AI stores its records in the United States. If you use it from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States, and our service providers may process it in other countries under their own safeguards.
